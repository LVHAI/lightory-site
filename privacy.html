Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Lightory ("we", "us", "the app") handles your information. Lightory is a single-device screen lighting tool. Core features work offline. We do not require an account, we do not operate a backend, and we do not upload your photos or videos.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or contacts
  • Your location
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA) or any tracking identifier
  • Any biometric template, facial embedding, or Face ID data
  • Your photos or videos
  • Any analytics or usage telemetry

2. Photos and Camera

Camera access is requested only when you open a camera feature (Selfie Studio, Product Studio, Makeup Studio, or AI Lighting Coach). Photos and videos you capture are saved to your photo library only when you tap Save. We do not upload any captured media.

3. Face Data and Camera Permission (Face Data Policy)

AI Lighting Coach uses Apple Vision to detect face rectangles on-device in order to suggest a lighting recipe. This section explains exactly what happens to that data.

Purpose

Face rectangles are used only to generate a lighting recommendation (color, brightness, color temperature, phone placement hint) for the photo you selected.

Processing Location

All processing happens locally on your device using Apple Vision and on-device image statistics. No external server, cloud service, or third-party AI API is involved.

Storage and Retention

Face coordinates and the temporary photo file you selected are discarded immediately after analysis. Retention time is 0 seconds. No face data is written to the app database, logs, crash reports, or any remote server.

Sharing

Face data is never shared with third parties, advertisers, or any external service.

Biometric Templates

We do not generate, store, or transmit Face ID data, facial meshes, face embeddings, or any biometric template.

Training

Face data is not used for model training.

How to Opt Out

Simply do not enter AI Lighting Coach. You can also revoke camera or photo access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.

Summary: Face detection runs in memory only. The photo you pick is deleted after analysis. No upload, no logging, no retention.

4. Purchases

Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase processed entirely by Apple. We read your purchase status from StoreKit to unlock Pro features. We store only a local UI cache of your entitlement state; the authoritative source is always StoreKit. We never see your payment method, billing address, or Apple ID password.

5. Data Retention

  • Lighting recipes and app preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Temporary photo files created for AI analysis are deleted after analysis completes, fails, or is cancelled.
  • Uninstalling Lightory removes all local data from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Lightory does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app does not collect personal information at all, it is safe for general audiences.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@lightory.app.
