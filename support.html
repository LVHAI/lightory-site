Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?

Open Lightory, go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy Pro.

Does the AI Lighting Coach upload my photos?

No. All analysis runs on your device using Apple Vision. Your photos never leave your phone, and no account is required.

Why does the screen light look different on my device?

Screen color and brightness depend on your iPhone model, display settings (True Tone, Night Shift, auto-brightness), and ambient light. For the most consistent results, turn off True Tone and Night Shift while using Lightory.

Can Lightory replace a professional ring light or light box?

No. Lightory is a fill-light helper. It works best in dim rooms and for close-up shots. For professional product photography, a dedicated light box is still recommended.

How do I import or export lighting recipes?

Go to Recipes → My Recipes and tap the menu in the top right. Import and export use standard JSON files, so you can share recipes with other Lightory users.

I found a bug. Where should I report it?

Please email support@lightory.app with a short description, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. Screenshots help a lot.
